Tingrui Shen

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at South China University of Technology and an incoming master's student at Peking University. My work focuses on 3D generation, world models, and embodied AI.

I have worked with research groups at Singapore Management University, Peking University, and Huazhong University of Science and Technology, and with industry teams at Tencent and VAST/Tripo3D on 3D mesh generation, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence.
